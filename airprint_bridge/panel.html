Maintenance

These are community service commands, not Epson-certified controls. The app checks the printer model before sending a command. No maintenance runs automatically.

Power cleaning consumes substantial ink and adds waste ink. It is not verified as the ET-2500's full “Power Ink Flushing” procedure. For that procedure use Epson's instructions.

Waste-ink pad counter reset

Service or replace the physical waste-ink pads first. Resetting the counter does not clean them. The app saves the relevant counter bytes and verifies writes, but a firmware-specific command may still fail. Counter reset is untested on this printer.
